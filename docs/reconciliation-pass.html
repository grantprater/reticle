The Reconciliation Pass

The pipeline has forked in three places while nobody was looking, and the checks written to catch exactly that could not see any of them. This is what forked, what the arbitration says, and the order I would fix it in — followed by the work that comes next once the trunk is single again.

§1 The result that reorders the queue

floor_mask exists twice, and the two implementations disagree. reticle/minimap.py gates on sat < 60 & val > 110 and dilates; prototypes/minimap_icons.py gates on sat < 20 & val > 100, takes the largest connected component, and bridges nearby ones. CLAUDE.md records this fork as byte-identical and therefore harmless. That note is stale. They are 9 lines and 61 lines, and they have been diverging since 2026-08-27.

the two painted map masks settle it. Both are unseeded, both predate the current form of either mask, and neither was made for this question — which is the good case.

The prototype mask wins, and the shipped mask's perfect recall is an artefact

The prototype's mask is a near-strict subset of the shipped one — it adds 0.01% on Ascent and 0.11% on Lotus, and none of that lands inside the painting. So the whole question is what the shipped mask adds on top, and on Ascent that extra 15.2% of the widget is 90.1% outside the painting. It is the semi-transparent void, which is the single documented cause of reading non-minimap content as icons. Shipped scores 100% recall on both sessions because a superset of the answer trivially contains the answer; IoU is the column that means anything, and it is 74.8 against 57.8.

Two honest caveats, because this changes stored numbers. First, the painting is the searchable area and floor_mask is the opaque slab — related targets, not identical ones, and the verdict on the first painting was that's not even close to pixel perfect … but that's the gist. Second, I could not reproduce the prototype docstring's claim that sat < 60 "took 83% of the ROI". It takes 51.5%. That number is itself stale, which is the disease rather than a second opinion.

And the prototype mask has its own defect, now localised

The 5.1% of Ascent it loses is not scattered edge pixels — it is two blobs, 2355 px and 1059 px, plus three on Lotus. Sweeping BRIDGE from 25 to 60 moves recall 94.9% → 95.3% and then costs precision, so these are not rooms cut off by a narrow gap. They fail sat < 20 & val > 100 outright. Render those two blobs and ask the player what they are; that is a one-minute question for the player and an afternoon of derivation for anyone else. It is the same shape as the Boathouse finding that produced BRIDGE in the first place.

§2 What forked, and why nothing caught it

Three forks, one mechanism. In each case a thing was added and the thing it superseded was left running.

The duplicate-def check cannot see the fork it was written for. git grep -h "^def " | sort | uniq -d matches on the whole signature line, and the two are floor_mask(med) and floor_mask(med, dilate=9). The check passes clean. It is the built_by lesson one level further out: the artefact was stamped, the code path was not, and then the check on the code path was written to match text rather than meaning.

Three more instances of the same drift, none urgent on its own and all pointing the same way:

  • 19 of 70 prototypes are named by no other file and no document. The 2026-08-26 enemy-teacher cluster (~2,400 lines) is the part that reads as genuinely superseded rather than pending;
  • persistence has forked four ways. The L0/L1/L2 design covers three tables — hud, minimap, primitives. Pings and abilities go to events/*.jsonl, casts to casts/, series to series/, rosters to rosters/, and the readable scoreboard goes nowhere. Version stamps, recompute triggers and metrics comparability only reach the parquet three, so every channel added since has landed outside the machinery built to keep channels honest;
  • "shipped" has two meanings. prototypes/CLAUDE.md calls minimap_ring_fit.py the SHIPPED enemy finder; no module in reticle/ imports it, and reticle/minimap.py's docstring says enemy icons are out of scope. It means chosen, not in the pipeline.
The ratio worth staring at

prototypes/ is 21,479 lines against reticle/'s 9,772 — 2.2 to 1 — and it holds code the docs call shipped. Alongside it sits 3,956 lines of prose across the two CLAUDE.md files and NOTES.md. Neither ratio is wrong for an exploratory phase. Both mean that "which tree is the truth" has to be answered by a rule rather than by convention, because convention has now failed at it three times.

§3 The rule I would adopt, and how to make it refuse

The existing rule — a promoted function is DELETED from its prototype and re-exported, never copied — is correct and was violated anyway, because nothing enforced it. The repo's own conversion audit already predicts this: every convention encoded as code that refuses has held, and every one written as prose has recurred. So the fix is not a better sentence.

One name, one implementation, one tree. If a symbol is defined in reticle/, defining it again anywhere in prototypes/ is an error. Not a warning, not a note. That is checkable in about fifteen lines of AST walk, and it catches all three forks above plus the ones not yet made.

I would fold that into a single command rather than a git hook, because a hook that fires on commit is a hook that gets bypassed under time pressure — which is precisely the condition under which the last three recurrences happened.

reticle doctor            # every structural check, one exit code

  DUPLICATE   floor_mask defined in reticle/minimap.py and
              prototypes/minimap_icons.py  -- one name, one implementation
  UNWIRED     reticle/refine.py, reticle/roster.py reachable from no CLI command
  ORPHAN      19 prototypes named by no code and no document
  STALE       3 sessions at minimap-0.1.0; code is at minimap-0.2.0
  DONOR       2ba870ccbd50 is tagged small-widget and shares a
              large-widget donor's static map

Every one of those lines is a fact I had to derive by hand today, and every one will need deriving again next month. reticle status already proved the principle for the store — a fact that is computed cannot disagree with the code. This is the same move applied to the repo.

The one convention I would try hardest to convert

Align the window to the question has five recorded recurrences and is the most repeated mistake in the codebase. It cannot be caught by types, because the wrong span returns a plausible number of the right type. But it can be caught by refusal: a small Window(t0, t1, why) value that every aggregate takes and echoes into its own output. That does not stop you choosing the wrong window — nothing can — but it makes every printed figure carry the span it was computed over, which is the second of the three tells the convention already names. A number without a window becomes unprintable rather than merely unchecked.

The cheapest unexploited idea in the repo

The per-region temporal SD map — 7.4 on white lines, 16.9 on the slab, 42.5 in the void — is, up to a constant, a map of how much world bleeds through each pixel. It is currently used once, as an argument, computed last to explain a conclusion after five derived masks had failed. Soft weight instead of hard gate, per session, from frames already decoded. This is the one item on this page whose ceiling I genuinely cannot guess.

§6 One question that changes the architecture

The endstate says real-time labelling of a VOD. Everything downstream has read that as "runs over a whole match at a usable rate", and that reading may well be right — but it has never been put back to the player, and the two readings build different systems.

Nothing in the current design streams. The static map is a per-pixel median over ~120 seeks spread across the session; spans come from segmenting the whole file; every minimap detector depends on the median existing first. If real-time means live-while-playing, the median becomes an incremental warm-up estimate and the first round is read with a worse map than the last — a real, permanent accuracy cost that should be chosen deliberately rather than discovered in month three. If it means offline-but-fast, then throughput is a metric to record and nothing structural changes.

ask, do not derive This is a question, not a finding. It is on this page because it is the highest-leverage sentence the player could say in the next session, and because the project's own convention says to ask the player on the first failure rather than the fifth.

§7 Lower expected value, kept on the page anyway

Asked for explicitly, and worth writing down because the cheap ones are cheap enough that being wrong about them costs an hour. Roughly descending by my own estimate of value, which §4 of the judgement log suggests should be read with suspicion.

The clock is a known dynamical system

It decrements at exactly 1 Hz. It is the only field in the entire pipeline whose dynamics are known exactly, and it is being read independently per frame at a 33–60% success rate with a documented transient-extra-digit defect on 9acf02f98283. A one-dimensional filter with a known rate makes every neighbour-inconsistent read detectable, and probably correctable. This is on the low-EV list only because I have not checked whether rounds.py already absorbs the damage; if it does not, this is not low EV at all.

Composite your own training data

The widget is α·widget + (1−α)·world, α is fixed and unexposed, you have static maps for the widget term and endless main-view frames for a plausible world term. So labelled glyph data can be synthesised at known positions, known α, known occlusion — unlimited exact labels for the problem currently starved at 85 positives. The synthetic-to-real gap is the risk and it is a real one, but the control is free: score a synthetic-fit detector against the existing painted frames and you learn the size of the gap rather than assuming it.

Buy a second regime for one session

Ask the player to record one match with the enemy outline set to yellow instead of red, and one with visibly different brightness. Every hue and level constant in the repo is currently "right for these captures"; two sessions convert the whole set from unmeasured to measured-across-two-regimes, and the enemy-outline band is explicitly documented as a per-profile value the moment a capture uses a different setting. Cheapest insurance on the page.

Golden frames in the repo

teststore/ half-exists already. A dozen committed frames with hand-checked answers turn "re-run it and confirm a known number comes back" from a convention into a command that takes four seconds. The convention it replaces has one recorded near-catastrophe behind it — a gutted file that passed ast.parse.

Matched filtering for pings

Four classes, fixed size, no growth phase, free labels from a clip the player already recorded, and a documented finding that a colour threshold cannot work because warm Sunset scenery floods it. That is close to a textbook normalised-cross-correlation problem, and wiki_map.py already has NCC in the repo — used for map alignment, never for glyphs.

Skip stage 01's classifier

The design doc wants a trained buy/in-round/post-round/menu/spectate classifier and there is no labelled data for one. But the spike graphic gives post-plant, the roster gives alive counts and therefore round boundaries, the clock gives phase, and the combat report gives spectating. Four deterministic signals may make the classifier unnecessary — and if they do not, they are its features and its labels.

Ship the film retrieval early

CLAUDE.md calls this the highest value per unit of effort in the whole project and then schedules it last, behind WPA. A ranked list of moments with timestamps does not actually need the win-probability model to be useful — rounds you died first in, rounds where the alive count went 5v5 to 2v5 inside ten seconds, and every entry the killfeed and roster disagreed about are all computable today from stored L1, and all three are things the player would watch. It is a way to find out whether the delivery mechanism works before spending months on the thing it delivers.

Delete more than feels comfortable

Genuinely low EV and listed for completeness. The 2,400-line enemy-teacher cluster, the four superseded portrait variants, the ability_* files the corpus re-founding orphaned. Nothing breaks by leaving them; the cost is that the next session reads them as live and copies from them, which is the mechanism that produced the floor_mask fork in the first place. git is the archive — that is what it is for.

§8 What I would not do

  • Not shrink CLAUDE.md for its own sake. It is 1,293 lines and every attempt to describe it as bloat runs into the fact that its domain notes are the least replaceable thing in the repo. The problem is not length, it is that prose sections claim enforcement they do not have — which §3 fixes by moving checks into code, and which shortens the file as a side effect rather than as a goal.
  • Not chase the remaining killfeed divergences. One read error and two unexplained across 372 events, with the definitional-divergence rule now doing the explaining. That is past the point where more tuning is measurement rather than improvement.
  • Not raise any sample rate until the two-pass design is real. Event timing is explicitly not a constraint on the main extractors, and a raised rate is the most expensive way to buy something the architecture already says you do not need.
  • Not add shape features to the ability bank. Measured near-redundant, and the equal-weight z-sum already beats the weighted combiner. This is in NOTES.md as a standing "do not" and it belongs here too, because a plan that does not repeat its own prohibitions gets them re-litigated.
