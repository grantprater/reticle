# Ultimate voice lines

Can the game's own ultimate voice lines, matched as templates against the
match audio, say which ultimate was cast, by which agent, on which side and
when? The audio gate's review found that its unexplained detections were
allies' casts with their voice lines ([audio gate](AUDIO_GATE.md#the-players-review-2026-09-27)),
and the game announces casts in fixed lines
[domain:abilities/voice-lines-announce-casts]. A fixed asset is a template,
not a class to learn. The prototype is `prototypes/voice_lines.py`; it emits
no events, writes nothing under `events/` or `labels/`, and `reticle/` does
not import it.

## The question and the bootstrap

The store holds the official ultimate lines,
`reference/assets/voicelines/<Agent>_ult_{ally,enemy}.mp3`, two variants per
agent: the caster's allies hear the ally variant and the caster's enemies hear
the enemy variant. Which variant fires therefore carries the caster's side.

No human label is needed to measure false alarms. The session's lineup makes
most templates impossible: the ally variant of an agent not on the player's
team, the enemy variant of an agent the enemy did not field. A
detection of an impossible template is a false alarm unless the lineup is
wrong. The player's own ult casts, dated by the tray's X-slot drops, test
whether the caster hears their own line, which no stored fact settles; an agent
does not speak every line on every use
[domain:abilities/ability-voice-line-mask].

## Data, all stored

- Session log-mel: `analysis/audio-gate/0.1.0/features/<sid>.npz`, cached by
  the audio gate for the 20 match sessions and 5 ability demos.
- Lineups: `reticle.lineup.load_lineup`, whose `agent_identity` verdicts are
  the identity arbiter's names per slot. The loader applies the stored
  scoreboard's side sets when they are current. With that constraint the
  arbiter names every slot of both sides on each session with a lineup (see
  "What ran"). One match, `c62c2b06bcfb`, has no lineup; every template there is `unknown`.
- The player's agent: the arbiter's resolved verdict for the player's slot
  (`audio_bank.player_agent`); on a demo, the manifest's agent tag.
- Own X casts: `events/tray_drop` X-slot drops that the owner accepts
  (`player_cast`, computed by `ability_timeline.player_tray_casts`) and does
  not mark suspect, or that the player labelled in `labels/tray_object`, the
  audio gate's own-cast rule restricted to the X slot. The verified subset is
  the labelled X rows. On the demos: census X rows the eye check kept, and Run
  it Back on `6afc32cb46b4` at 43.2 s, dated from decoded frames because the
  tray does not show it [domain:abilities/phoenix-run-it-back-expiry-flash].
- Live time: gametime's `round_live` and `post_plant` over the rounds table,
  dead or alive, because an ult line sounds while the player spectates;
  stalled spans (`reticle.stalls`) are excluded. Rounds decide the round of a
  detection through `rounds.in_round_window`.
- Podcast sessions: the six above the audio gate's speech cut
  (`analysis/audio-gate/0.1.0/report/speech.json`).

## Method as fixed

- **Front end.** `audio_gate.logmel`, the function that produced the session
  features: 64 mel bands from 60 Hz to 16 kHz, 2048-point frames at 48 kHz, a
  10 ms hop. Each mp3 is decoded with PyAV and resampled to 48 kHz mono by
  libswresample (Deadlock's and Iso's lines are 44.1 kHz stereo).
- **Template preparation.** Frames whose window reaches outside the clip
  leave. The template spans the first to the last frame within 40 dB of its
  loudest frame's power, and values more than 50 dB below its maximum rise to
  that floor, so digital silence in the asset cannot demand silence of the
  match. The template's first kept frame is the line's onset; every score is
  placed at the onset.
- **F-A, log-mel correlation.** The template less its per-band mean, at unit
  norm, against the session log-mel less its per-band session median; the
  window's own per-band means are removed too, so the score is the cosine of
  the two centred patches. A window whose centred RMS falls below 1 dB per
  element (undecoded audio, digital silence) scores 0. FFT on the GPU (cupy).
- **F-B, whitened waveform correlation (GCC-PHAT).** The capture's audio
  stream is decoded in memory with `audio_gate.decode_mono` (no video, no
  file written) and cut into chunks of 2^20 samples that overlap by the
  longest template. Each chunk's cross spectrum with the template waveform
  (the same kept span) is divided by its magnitude between 100 Hz and 8 kHz
  and zeroed outside; the inverse FFT, scaled so a perfect match reads 1, is
  the score at each lag, and each 10 ms frame keeps its largest lag score.
- **F-C, gain-free log-mel correlation.** F-A after removing each frame's
  mean over bands, template and session alike.
- **Peaks.** Local maxima at or above the 99th percentile of that template's
  track on that session, greedy by score, none within one template length of
  a higher one. Every threshold below is applied to these peaks; the report
  checks that the operating threshold lies above every stored floor.
- **Classes.** Per session and template: `own`, the player's agent's ally
  variant; `possible`, the ally variant of a named ally or the enemy variant
  of a named enemy; `impossible`, the ally variant of an agent neither named
  on the ally side nor the best guess or rival of an unresolved ally slot, and
  the enemy variant of an agent absent from a fully named enemy side;
  `unknown`, the rest.
- **Operating point.** Pooled over the match sessions with a lineup, the
  lowest threshold at which impossible-template detections in live time run
  at most 0.1 per live minute. One threshold per formulation.
- **Scores at the operating point.** Own recall: an own X cast is found when
  an own-template detection lies within 1.5 s of its drop; the same on the
  verified X casts. Onset minus drop over the found casts (the tray samples
  at 2 Hz, so a drop trails its cast by up to 0.5 s). Possible detections per
  live minute, ally and enemy variants apart. The enemy-to-ally ratio per
  session (enemy-variant possible detections over ally-variant own and
  possible detections), median over sessions. The round invariant: the share
  of detections that are the only one of their agent and variant in their
  round. The impossible rate per session, podcast sessions marked, and the
  podcast-to-rest ratio at the operating threshold and at the threshold where
  the pooled impossible rate is 1 per live minute. Per-agent detection
  counts against the lineup. The share of impossible detections within 1.5 s
  of a higher possible or own detection measures cross-talk: one line firing
  several templates.
- **Review.** Thirty detections at the operating point, stratified: own
  lines, possible ally lines, possible enemy lines, the five highest possible
  or own peaks just below the threshold, and five impossible ones, each with
  a spectrogram (1.5 s before to 3 s after the onset) and an `ffplay`
  command into the source capture beside one that plays the official line.
