**Priority: ideate and experiment on the adjudicator as a statistical model (player, 2026-09-23).** Icon position, facing and cone geometry are not measured precisely enough to explain marginal observations such as cone slivers exactly. The adjudicator's job is to ask, cheaply, whether a plausible measurement error explains another observation: if the self facing were off by a few degrees, or the position by a pixel or two, would the cone then cover this sliver? Carry each measurement with its uncertainty, and make the causal, temporal and interpolation rules likelihoods over those uncertainties rather than hard gates. The player prescribes no technique. Start with ideation: candidate formulations, what each needs stored, and a falsifiable first experiment scored on the grouping labels and the three slivers. Keep refusals as refusals when no plausible error explains an observation. Map geometry is an uncertain input too: a wall edge off by a pixel explains a sliver as well as a facing error does. Slivers are also evidence for iteratively improving the geometry, the lit-region reader and the icon detectors. Geometry refinements pool evidence across sessions into the geometry builder under a new stamp, never a per-session correction, and any refinement is scored on held-out slivers or an independent witness, because tuning on the observations the model already explained grades its own homework.
