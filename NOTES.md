**The prototype.** `reticle/pipeline.py` runs a staged pass. The dispatcher, the calling thread, reads the source and offers each frame read-only; at one worker or more, each reader feeds on its own thread behind a bounded FIFO, and at workers 0 the pass feeds inline in the serial order. A reader that declares `shardable` (today `AllyIconReader`) splits by producer position, frame n to shard n mod K, and the merge restores frame order. `scan` gains `--pipeline staged`, `--workers`, `--shard`, `--cv-threads`, `--check`, `--check-dir` and `--until`. Usage records become `scan-usage-2`, with CPU per process and per thread, and every scan appends a `scan_usage` pass row to `notes/metrics.jsonl`, so QUOTED can cite a scan.
